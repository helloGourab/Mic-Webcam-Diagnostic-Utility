## Host Production Notes & Delivery Guidelines

| Attribute | Guideline |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Pacing** | Moderate-fast (130–145 words per minute) with deliberate pauses before URL callouts. |
| **Tone** | Relatable, technical yet accessible, peer-to-peer recommendation (not a cheesy infomercial). |
| **Pronunciation** | *dBFS* = "D-B-F-S" or "decibels full scale"; *FFT* = "F-F-T" (Fast Fourier Transform). |
| **URL Cadence** | Spell out clearly: *"mic-cam-tester.com"* at the midpoint and twice at the conclusion. |
| **Sound Design Cue (Optional)** | Insert a clean stereo test tone (Left channel ping then Right channel ping) during the 60s read if audio production allows. |
